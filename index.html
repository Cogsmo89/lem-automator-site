FAQ

Common questions, answered upfront.

How long does an automation project take?

Small jobs (1–3 workflows): 3–7 days. Mid‑size systems (CRM + AI agents + integrations): 2–4 weeks. Larger builds get a phased plan so you ship value every week, not at the end.

Do you work with my existing stack, or do I have to switch?

I work with what you have. n8n, Make.com, Zapier, GoHighLevel, custom APIs — whatever's already running. If a swap genuinely saves money or time I'll flag it, but it's never required.

How is pricing structured?

Most projects are scoped fixed‑fee after a free audit. For ongoing automation operations, monthly retainers are available. You'll never get a surprise invoice.

Will my team be able to maintain it after launch?

Yes. Every build comes with a Loom walkthrough, a written SOP, and clean naming so your team can read what's happening without me. I also offer optional monthly maintenance.

Can you build AI voice or chat agents that use our real data?

That's most of what I do. RAG over your internal docs, knowledge bases, or live CRM data — so the AI answers with your truth, not generic LLM guesses.

What time zones do you cover?

Based in Mandaue City, Philippines. Working hours 8 AM – 8 PM EST, which overlaps cleanly with US, UK, EU, AU and Asia clients.

How do we get started?

Book a free 30‑minute call on Google Meet using the button above. I'll audit your current workflow live, point out 2–3 quick wins, and only propose a project if it makes sense.
