Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 April 2026

1. The short version

We don't track you. lemautomator.work doesn't set marketing or advertising cookies. The only cookies that may appear are essential ones from our infrastructure (Cloudflare, Vercel) and any third‑party services you actively interact with (e.g., Google Calendar).

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site can save in your browser. They can remember preferences, keep you signed in, or help operators understand how a site is used. Some cookies are essential for a site to work; others are optional.

3. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary

  • Cloudflare — security cookies (e.g., __cf_bm, cf_clearance) used to distinguish humans from bots and keep the site online.
  • Vercel — load‑balancing and security cookies set automatically by our hosting provider.

These cannot be disabled without breaking the site. They don't track you across other sites.

Functional (only when you interact)

  • Google Calendar — when you click the booking button, Google may set its own cookies in their booking interface. These are governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Embedded social links — clicking social media links sends you to those platforms, which set their own cookies. We don't embed trackers from them on this site.

Analytics & advertising

  • None. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other behavioural tracking.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies any time through your browser:

  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of this site. For optional/third‑party cookies, you can also use browser features like "Do Not Track", private browsing, or extensions like uBlock Origin.

5. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as our infrastructure evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects any change.
